Cattle & Farming

The quality begins
long before
the product.

The nutritional quality of dairy is directly determined by how the animal lives. A stressed cow on poor feed produces milk that reflects that. Our animals live on balanced diets, in open environments, with the care that both Vedic tradition and modern animal science prescribe.

We work with both own and partner farms within Nagpur district — each evaluated for welfare standards, feeding practices, and hygiene systems before we source from them.

Indigenous Cow
Gir
Gujarat Origin

One of India's oldest and most revered indigenous breeds. Produces pure A2 beta-casein milk. Known for high fat content and deep nutritional profile.

Indigenous Cow
Sahiwal
Punjab Origin

Among the highest milk-yielding indigenous breeds. Exceptional heat tolerance and disease resistance. A2 milk with rich nutritional composition.

Premium Buffalo
Zafrabadi
Gujarat Origin

One of India's largest buffalo breeds. Produces milk exceptionally high in fat — ideal for rich dairy products with superior nutritional density.

Premium Buffalo
Murrah
Haryana Origin · Black Gold

The world's highest milk-yielding buffalo breed. Called "Black Gold" for its premium milk quality. Superior fat and protein profile in every batch.

Milk from our Zafrabadi & Murrah buffalo becomes our Raw A2 Buffalo Milk — 100% raw, tested daily, zero additives.

🌿
Balanced Natural Diet

Seasonal grasses, green fodder, and carefully formulated feed supplements. No low-cost filler feed. What the animal eats directly determines what ends up in the milk — and we take that seriously.

☀️
Open Environment

No confined stalls. Animals move freely across open farm areas. Physical activity and low-stress environments are proven to influence milk composition positively.

🐄
Calf-First Milking

The calf feeds first, always. We collect only the natural surplus after the calf is satisfied. This traditional practice ensures the animal is not over-milked and the calf receives its complete nutrition.

💉
No Growth Hormones

Zero synthetic hormones to artificially boost yield. Our volumes are smaller because our animals are not stressed into producing more than is natural. That restraint shows in the quality.

🛡️
Breed Preservation

Every purchase supports indigenous Indian cattle breeds that commercial dairy nearly wiped out in a single generation. Gir and Sahiwal populations declined sharply after HF/Jersey crossbreeding was incentivised. We are part of bringing them back.

🔍
Open Farm Policy

Our farms are not a trade secret. Visit us. Watch the milking. Meet the animals. We believe radical transparency about sourcing is not just ethical — it is the strongest brand statement we can make.

🧒
Child Growth & Development

High-bioavailability protein, natural calcium, and live probiotic cultures support bone development, immune function, and gut health in children. Quality over quantity — always.

🧘
Lactose Sensitivity

A2 milk still contains lactose, so it isn't a fix for true lactose intolerance. But some people who feel discomfort after regular milk find A2 milk and live-culture dairy easier on digestion, since research suggests A1 beta-casein — not lactose alone — may be behind some of that discomfort. If you're sensitive, we'd suggest trying a small amount first.

Transparency Centre

We have nothing
to hide.
Here is everything.

Transparency is not a section on our website. It is the foundation of everything we do. The standards below are not aspirational — they describe what we do every single day. Ask us to verify any of them.

Ask us anything →
🌱
No Milk Solids — Ever

We never add milk solids to bulk up our products. Every gram you pay for is genuine dairy from our animals — nothing else.

🎨
No Artificial Flavoring

The flavour in our products comes entirely from the quality of the source milk, the breed, and the process. No artificial enhancement of any kind.

💉
No Synthetic Hormones

Our animals are never injected with rBST or any synthetic growth hormones to artificially boost milk yield. Our volumes reflect natural production.

🔬
Antibiotic Discipline

When veterinary treatment requires antibiotics, that animal's milk is kept out of our supply for the mandatory withdrawal period. No exceptions.

🌡️
Chilled Within 30 Minutes

Every collection is chilled to below 4°C within 30 minutes of milking. Cold chain integrity from farm to your door — not approximated, maintained.

📋
Batch Records Available

Every product batch has a source record. Ask us about any product you've received — we can trace it. This accountability is intentional.
